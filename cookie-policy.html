Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Solera Cask (Asta Xera S.L.) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website in compliance with Spanish Law 34/2002 on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (LSSI-CE) and EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, improve user experience, and provide information to website owners.

Cookies can be "session cookies" (which are deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain on your device for a set period).

2. Types of Cookies We Use

These cookies are essential for our website to function properly. They cannot be disabled as they are necessary for core functionality.

2.2 Analytics Cookies (Optional - Requires Consent)

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website, allowing us to improve our service. You can opt out of these cookies through our cookie preference center.

Note: Analytics cookies are not currently active on our website. If we implement them in the future, we will update this policy and request your consent.

3. Form Spam Protection (No Cookies)

To protect our contact and quote request forms from spam and automated abuse, we use Netlify Forms with honeypot protection. This is a server-side spam prevention technique that:

  • Does not set any cookies on your device
  • Does not track your browsing activity
  • Uses hidden form fields that are invisible to human users but detected by bots
  • Processes spam detection entirely on our servers

Provider: Netlify, Inc.
Privacy Policy: https://www.netlify.com/privacy/

This spam protection method is privacy-friendly and does not require your consent as it does not involve cookies or tracking technologies.

4. Purpose of Cookie Usage

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential functionality: Enable core website features like form submissions
  • Preferences: Remember your cookie consent choices
  • Analytics (with consent): Understand how visitors use our website to improve user experience
