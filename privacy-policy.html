Privacy Policy

2. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following personal data through our quote request forms:

Mandatory Information:

  • First name
  • Last name
  • Email address
  • Brewery/Distillery name
  • Estimated quantity of barrels

Optional Information:

  • Phone number
  • Shipping address
  • Sherry seasoning preference
  • Cask size preference
  • Project details

3. Legal Basis and Purpose

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Contractual necessity: To process your quote requests, prepare proposals, and execute contracts for barrel sales
  • Legitimate business interest: To maintain business records and communicate with clients about orders and services
  • Consent: For sending marketing communications (newsletters) - only if you explicitly opt-in
  • Legal obligation: To comply with Spanish tax and commercial record-keeping requirements

4. How We Use Your Data

Your personal data is used exclusively for:

  • Processing and responding to your quote requests
  • Preparing commercial proposals and contracts
  • Managing orders and coordinating shipments
  • Maintaining business records for legal and tax purposes
  • Communicating with you about your inquiries and orders
  • Sending newsletters and updates (only with your explicit consent)

We will never use your data for purposes other than those specified without obtaining your prior consent.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using the following systems:

  • Email System: Quote form submissions are sent to and stored in our Apple iCloud Mail system, which complies with GDPR and maintains data centers in the EU
  • Website Hosting: Our website is hosted on Netlify, with infrastructure in compliance with international security standards

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Data Retention Period

We retain your personal data for the following periods:

  • Quote requests: Up to 6 years from the date of inquiry, in compliance with Spanish commercial and tax law requirements
  • Contract and order data: 6 years from contract completion, as required by Spanish tax authorities
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Until you unsubscribe or request deletion

After these periods, your data will be securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Data Sharing and Transfers

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • Logistics partners: When necessary to coordinate shipments (only with your authorization and for this specific purpose)
  • Payment processors: When processing payments (only transaction-related information)
  • Legal requirements: When required by Spanish or EU law, or by court order
  • Email service provider: If you opt-in to newsletters, your email address will be processed by our newsletter service provider (Brevo or similar GDPR-compliant service)

All third-party service providers are carefully selected and contractually required to comply with GDPR and maintain appropriate security measures.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): Request deletion of your data when no longer necessary for the purposes collected
  • Right to restriction of processing: Request limitation of how we process your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent for newsletter subscriptions at any time
  • Right to lodge a complaint: File a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@soleracask.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies to ensure proper functionality and improve user experience. For detailed information about the cookies we use and how to manage them, please see our Cookie Policy.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data is primarily stored and processed within the European Union. If data transfers outside the EU become necessary, we will ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions for countries with equivalent data protection standards
  • Your explicit consent when required

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on our website with a new "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
